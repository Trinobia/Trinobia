# Trinobia

**Data-driven Bioinformatics Solutions.**

Trinobia is a bioinformatics company focused on delivering **high-impact, data-driven solutions** and **hands-on training** for biomedical researchers. We help research teams turn complex biological data into actionable insight — reproducibly, transparently, and at scale.

---

## 🧬 What We Do

We work at the intersection of **Bioinformatics, Genetics, and Training**.

**Services**
- Bioinformatics analysis for genomics, transcriptomics, and multi-omics data  
- Custom pipelines and workflows (reproducible & scalable)  
- Statistical modeling and data visualization for biomedical research  
- Research-grade software and analysis tooling  

**Training**
- Practical bioinformatics training for researchers and labs  
- Workshops on data analysis, reproducible research, and best practices  
- One-on-one and team-based mentoring  

---

## 🧠 Our Philosophy

We believe that:
- **Biological insight comes from rigorous data analysis**
- **Reproducibility is non-negotiable**
- **Researchers deserve tools they can use, understand and trust**
- **Training empowers science to move faster and farther**

Our work emphasizes clean code, transparent methods, and biologically meaningful interpretation — not black boxes.
